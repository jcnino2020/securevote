# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

University students voting in student council elections; administrators setting up and managing those elections.

## Product Purpose

SecureVote lets eligible students sign in, review elections and candidates, cast a ballot, and receive a verification receipt. Administrators manage the election and review results and audit information.

## Operating Context

Students may use the site on a phone or desktop. The app includes public election information, account registration and sign-in, a voter portal, ballot selection and review, results, and an administrative portal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing PHP routes, server-side behavior, forms, roles, voting flow, result displays, security messages, and accessibility semantics. The application also includes candidate comparisons, announcements, an isolated fictional practice ballot, receipt recording checks, readiness checks, atomic CSV voter imports, and student issue reports. Existing voting and results policies remain authoritative.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the SecureVote name and recognizable logo mark. The visual direction chosen for this redesign is student friendly: approachable, energetic, and clear for first-time voters.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing application pages, interface copy, icons, and logo in this repository. Election counts and status are database driven.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious to eligible voters.
- Present election status and ballot choices clearly.
- Give administrators dense information without visual clutter.
- Make trust cues specific and readable.

## Election engagement

Published election timelines, account-scoped in-app reminders, administrator-reviewed candidate Q&A, English/Filipino navigation and core voting instructions, and printable owned receipts. Reminders refresh on visits. Milestone dates inform voters without overriding election or results rules.
